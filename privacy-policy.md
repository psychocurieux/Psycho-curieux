# Privacy Policy – Psycho Curieux

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Psycho Curieux is an educational content project focused on psychology, human behavior, and interesting facts.

## Information We Collect

Psycho Curieux does not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information from users through this website.

When TikTok services are used, certain information may be processed through TikTok's APIs solely to provide the application's publishing functionality.

## How Information Is Used

Any information accessed through TikTok's APIs is used only to provide the features of Psycho Curieux, including connecting an authorized TikTok account and publishing content requested through the service.

We do not sell personal information.

## Third-Party Services

Psycho Curieux may use TikTok services and APIs. Use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's own terms and privacy policies.

## Data Retention

Psycho Curieux only retains information when necessary to operate the service. Users may revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings.

## Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the operator of Psycho Curieux.
